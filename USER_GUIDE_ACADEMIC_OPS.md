# User Manual: Academic & Operations Workflow
## For Academic Leads / Excellence, SHO, and SSHO

This guide details the end-to-end workflow for managing batches and students, starting from batch creation by the Academic Team to verification by the Operations Team (SHO/SSHO).

---

## 🏗️ Phase 1: Batch Creation (Academic Excellence)

**Role**: `ACADEMIC_LEAD` (Academic Excellence)

The lifecycle of a new batch begins with the Academic Lead. You are responsible for defining the batch and ensuring it's ready for enrollments.

### Steps to Create a Batch:

1.  **Login** to the Batch Ascent Dashboard using your credentials.
2.  Navigate to the **"Create Batch"** section via the sidebar menu.
3.  **Fill in the Batch Details Form**:
    *   **Batch ID**: Enter a unique identifier for the batch (e.g., `N8N03`).
    *   **Batch Name**: Give it a descriptive name (e.g., `ChatGPT Gemini`).
    *   **Course Name**: Select the specific course from the categorical dropdown (e.g., *Tech > Applied AI*).
    *   **Orientation Date**: Select the start date associated with the orientation.
    *   **Batch Strength**: Define the maximum capacity (e.g., `50`).
    *   **SHO Name**: Select the **Student Happiness Officer (SHO)** responsible for this batch from the dropdown list.
    *   **School**: This is automatically filled based on your profile (e.g., *Tech School*).
    *   **Mode**: Choose between **Online** or **Offline**.
4.  Click **"Create Batch"**.

**Result**: The batch is now live. Sales teams can begin enrolling students into it, and the assigned SHO will see it in their dashboard.

## ✅ Phase 2: Operations & Verification (SHO / SSHO)

**Role**: `SHO` (Student Happiness Officer) / `SSHO` (Senior Student Happiness Officer)

Once a batch is created and Sales starts enrolling students, your goal is to verify these students and manage the batch capacity.

### 1. View Your Batches
*   Login to your Dashboard.
*   You will see a list of **Available Batches** assigned to your school.
*   The card displays:
    *   **Batch Name & ID**.
    *   **Start Date**.
    *   **Enrollment Status**: A progress bar showing how full the batch is (e.g., *12/50*).

### 2. Manage Student Enrollments
Click on a specific **Batch Card** to enter the detailed view. Here you will see a list of all students enrolled by sales.

#### **Understanding the Student Card:**
Each student is displayed as a card with a status indicator color:
*   🟧 **Orange Border**: **Pending verification**. Sales has added them, but **Sales Team Lead** hasn't confirmed them yet.
*   🟨 **Yellow Border**: **Verified**. **Sales Team Lead** has confirmed their enrollment.
*   🟩 **Green Border**: **Onboarded**. The **Student** has filled the onboarding form.

#### **Your Actions:**
1.  **Call the Student**:
    *   If you need to contact the student, click the **"Call"** button.
    *   This will open the dialer (on mobile) or show the number.
    *   **System Log**: The system automatically records that you called them (e.g., *"Called on 12/31/2024"*), preventing duplicate calls from other team members.

2.  **Verify the Student**:
    *   Once you have confirmed the student's details and payment status, click the **"Verify"** button.
    
    *   The card border will turn **Yellow**.
    *   This signals to the whole team that this student is a confirmed seat in the batch.

3.  **Check Cliq ID**:
    *   The student's Cliq Chat ID (if available) is displayed.
    *   If it shows "N/A" or seems outdated, click the **Refresh Icon** next to it to fetch the latest ID from their profile.

## 🎯 Summary of Responsibilities

| Role | Key Responsibility | Primary Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Academic Lead** | **Define the Product** | Create the Batch, Set the Course & Limits. |
| **Sales Team** | **Fill the Seats** | Enroll students into the batch (Generates Pending Cards). |
| **SHO / SSHO** | **Happiness & Verification** | Call & Verify students to confirm their seat. |
